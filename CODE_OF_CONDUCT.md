# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

The Sectum AI community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.
We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our standard

We expect all participants to:

- Be respectful, considerate, and constructive in all communication.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.
- Show empathy toward other community members.

Conduct that makes the community unwelcoming or unsafe for others is not
tolerated.

## Adoption of the Contributor Covenant

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its full code
of conduct — including its definitions of unacceptable conduct and its
enforcement guidelines. The complete text is published at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, and discussions — and in any public space when an individual is
representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainers at **conduct@sectum.ai**. All reports
are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security
of the reporter are respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct
and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any conduct
they deem inappropriate, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines.
